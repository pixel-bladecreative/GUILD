# Flex Payment Mortgage — Brand Guide for Ads

Source: flexpaymentmortgage.com (reviewed 2026-11-15). Flex Payment Mortgage is **Guild Mortgage's** suite of reverse-mortgage products (HECM, Refinance, Purchase, HomeSafe Seconds, Jumbo).

## Voice

Warm, plain-spoken and reassuring. It sells *flexibility and control*, not debt.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your mortgage, your way." | Lead with "reverse mortgage" in headlines |
| "your home is your key to financial flexibility" | "Free money", "tax-free cash", "never pay again" |
| "without selling your home" | Fear or urgency ("before it's too late") |
| "Our team is here to guide you every step of the way" | Jargon (HECM, LTV, principal limit) in headlines |
| "Financial peace of mind starts here" | Calling out age ("Are you over 62?"). Meta special ad category |

### Site phrases approved for reuse
- Your mortgage, your way.
- Your home is your key to financial flexibility.
- Make better use of your home equity without selling the home.
- Financial peace of mind starts here.
- Instantly find out how much you qualify for.
- Our team is here to guide you every step of the way.
- Get your Flex Payment Mortgage info kit.

### Message angles (from the site's "Popular uses")
1. **Payment freedom**: eliminate monthly mortgage payments*
2. **Income boost**: supplement income
3. **Home upgrades**: make home improvements
4. **Closer to family**: move closer to family / buy a home better suited for you
5. **Brand**: Your mortgage, your way

\*Always pair with: *Borrower must still pay property taxes, insurance and maintain the home.*

### Typography
The site uses **Circular Std** (Black/Bold/Medium/Book). It's a commercial Lineto font, so it isn't included here. For ads we substitute **Figtree** (OFL, `fonts/`), which is a close geometric match:
- Headlines: Figtree 800–900, tight tracking (-0.02em)
- "Flex Payment" wordmark-style accents: Figtree 800 italic
- Body/legal: Figtree 400–500

Swap in Circular Std if Guild provides the licensed files.

### Graphic motifs
- **Yellow triangle** (`assets/graphics/Yellow-triangle.png`): a large flat yellow triangle behind people, as in the site hero. In motion it works as a wipe or reveal.
- **Cyan arrow** (`assets/graphics/Flex-Payment-Arrow.png`): a forward motion cue, used to lead transitions and point at CTAs.
- Small yellow line icons (house, heart-house, check) for bullet lists.

### Photography
Bright, sunlit lifestyle photos of active, joyful older adults from many backgrounds, with lots of sky and air. Look for people outdoors, friends, and family across generations. Never use clinical, sad, or isolated imagery.

`assets/photos/` holds the site images. **Only the hero is high-resolution**; the others are ~800px and soft at 1080p+. **Check the usage license** before running them in paid media.

## Compliance (include on all paid creative)

- **End card / image footer (short):** `Guild Mortgage Company NMLS #3274. Equal Housing Opportunity. All loans subject to underwriter approval.`
- **Landing page / long copy:** `These materials are not from HUD or FHA and were not approved by HUD or a government agency.` Include it where space allows.
- Any mention of eliminating payments: add `Borrower must continue to pay property taxes, insurance and maintain the home.`
- Meta: run under **Special Ad Category: Financial products & services**, which means no age, gender or ZIP targeting. Don't put age in the copy.
