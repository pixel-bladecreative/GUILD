# 15-second spot scripts

**Shared end card (12–15s):** the Guild Flex Payment logo builds in (the cyan arrow draws, then the lockup appears), followed by the CTA pill, `flexpaymentmortgage.com · (877) 217-0166`, and the legal line *Guild Mortgage Company NMLS #3274. Equal Housing Opportunity. All loans subject to underwriter approval.*

## Production notes
- **Voice:** a warm, mature, trustworthy narrator, female or male. We'll pick from the ElevenLabs library and generate two takes per script.
- **Music:** gentle, uplifting acoustic guitar or piano at about 95 BPM with no vocals. Generated with SunoBoard, then trimmed to 15s with a 0.5s fade-out.
- **Mix:** voiceover at about −14 LUFS integrated, music ducked about −18dB under the voiceover.
- **Safe zones (9:16):** keep text out of the top 14% and bottom 35% (Reels UI). The end card legal line sits just above that bottom zone, or the legal text goes in the ad's primary text for Reels.
